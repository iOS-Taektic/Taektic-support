# Taektic — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

Taektic is a coaching tool for planning squads, tactics, training, and film
review. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

**Taektic does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.**
Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no
servers, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not connect to the
internet to send or receive your information. The only exception is buying
Taektic Pro, which Apple handles (see Purchases below).

## What data the app stores (on your device only)

You may enter information such as:

- Player names, ages, positions, attributes, and photos
- Team, roster, tactics, training, and schedule details
- Health/availability status and, if you use it, concussion-protocol
  records (incident notes, physician-clearance notes)
- Voice-over narration and video clips you record or import for film review

All of this is stored **locally** on your device (and in your own device
backups, e.g. iCloud/iTunes backups, which are controlled by your Apple
settings — not by Taektic). We never receive it.

## Device permissions

Taektic requests these only when you use the related feature, and the data
never leaves your device:

- **Camera** — to photograph a printed team sheet so the app can read the
  line-up.
- **Microphone** — to record voice-over narration onto your film clips.
- **Photos** — you choose photos/videos through Apple's system picker; the
  app only receives the items you pick. If you save an exported video, it is
  added to your photo library.

You can revoke any of these anytime in **Settings → Taektic**.

## Purchases

Taektic Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a yearly or monthly
subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment and
tells the app, on your device, whether you have Pro. Taektic never sees your
name, email address, or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is
covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Manage or cancel subscriptions in
your Apple Account settings.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers,
Apple may send Taektic anonymous crash reports. Taektic adds nothing to
them.

## Information about children

Coaches may record information about youth players, who may be minors.
Because Taektic stores this only on the coach's device and never transmits
or collects it, Taektic does not knowingly gather personal information from
children. If you enter data about a minor, you are responsible for handling
it in accordance with your local laws and your organization's policies,
including obtaining any required parental/guardian consent.

## Your control over the data

- **Export:** create a `.taektic` backup of your data at any time. Backups
  can be password-protected; Taektic cannot recover a lost password.
- **Delete:** remove individual players/records, or delete the app to
  remove all Taektic data from your device.

## Medical disclaimer

Any health or concussion features are for **record-keeping only** and are
**not medical advice**. They do not diagnose injuries or authorize return
to play. Always follow a qualified healthcare provider's evaluation and
your league's concussion protocol.

## Third-party components

The app includes an on-device machine-learning model (YOLOX, Apache-2.0)
for player detection. It runs entirely on your device and does not send any
footage or data anywhere.

The 3D view uses the Unity engine, which also runs entirely on your device.
Taektic does not use any Unity online service, and the 3D view sends no data
anywhere.
